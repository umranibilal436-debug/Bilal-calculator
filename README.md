## 🌟 Overview

## 📐 Mathematical Features

### 1. Basic Calculator Operations
- **Addition (`+`)**
- **Subtraction (`-`)**
- **Multiplication (`×`)**
- **Division (`÷`)**
- **Percentage (`%`)**
- **Decimal Point (`.`)**
- **Clear All (`AC`)**
- **Delete (`⌫`)**
- **Equals (`=`)**

### 2. Scientific Calculator Operations
- **Trigonometry:** Sine (`sin`), Cosine (`cos`), Tangent (`tan`)
- **Inverse Trigonometry:** ArcSine (`sin⁻¹`), ArcCosine (`cos⁻¹`), ArcTangent (`tan⁻¹`)
- **Angle Modes:** Degree (`DEG`) and Radian (`RAD`) mode toggle
- **Powers & Exponents:** Square (`x²`), Cube (`x³`), Power (`xʸ`), Exponential (`eˣ`), Power of 10 (`10ˣ`)
- **Roots:** Square Root (`√`), Cube Root (`∛`)
- **Logarithms:** Common Logarithm (`log` base 10), Natural Logarithm (`ln` base e)
- **Factorials:** (`n!`) for integers and fractional gamma approximations
- **Constants:** Pi (`π = 3.1415926535...`), Euler's Number (`e = 2.7182818284...`)
- **Grouping:** Open & Close Parentheses (`(`, `)`) with auto-closing
- **Reciprocal:** (`1/x`)
- **Absolute Value:** (`|x|`)
- **Negation:** (`±`)
- **Previous Answer:** (`Ans`)
- **Memory Operations:** `MC`, `MR`, `M+`, `M-`, `MS`

## ⚡ Unique Modes & Additional Features

### 1. Tactical Night Vision Mode
- Designed to preserve dark-adapted human scotopic night vision (rhodopsin protection).
- Activates pitch black background (`#000000`) and high-contrast tactical red LEDs (`#EF4444`).
- Perfect for astronomy, aviation, darkrooms, and late-night research.

### 2. Theme Options
- ☀️ **Light Mode (Default):** Pure White Website Background + Black Calculator Body + Blue Buttons.
- 🌙 **Dark Mode:** Titanium Dark Background + Black Body + Blue Buttons.
- 🔴 **Night Vision Mode:** Tactical Zero-Glare Scotopic Red HUD on Pitch Black.

### 3. Calculation History
- Slide-out drawer with persistent `localStorage` storage.
- Displays timestamps, full formulas, and results.
- 1-click to restore expression or reuse result.
- Export entire session history to `.txt`.

### 4. Live Dynamic Preview
- As formulas are typed, a real-time preview of the result updates instantly below the expression.

### 5. Web Audio Haptics
- Synthesizes crisp mechanical clicks and chime effects with zero external audio assets.
- Mute/unmute toggle available anytime.
